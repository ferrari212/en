## Engineering purpose
A digital vessel model is useful when its structure can be inspected, explained, and connected to engineering questions. Gunnerus, NTNU’s research vessel, provides a concrete example.

## My contribution
This visualization was featured in my original portfolio as part of my Vessel.js and ShipLab work. My contribution sits within that open-source collaboration; the complete vessel model and platform are collaborative work.

## Methods and implementation
A browser-based 3D interface allows switching between detailed and sketch views and interacting with major vessel components.

## Demonstrated capability
The model makes ship geometry and component relationships available for interactive exploration. For the step-by-step modelling explanation, see the [Vessel.js tutorial](/projects/vesseljs-tutorial/).
