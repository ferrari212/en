## Engineering purpose
The path from hull information to an executable simulation includes several modelling choices. An interactive explanation can make those steps easier to follow.

## My contribution
My original portfolio links this guide, published under my Observable account, as a complete visual and interactive introduction to creating digital vessel models with Vessel.js.

## Methods and implementation
The guide follows general-arrangement information and hull offset tables through digital modelling to a manoeuvrability simulation. It uses the Gunnerus example to keep the explanation grounded in a recognizable vessel.

## Demonstrated capability
The tutorial connects implementation with explanation: readers can follow how model inputs become an interactive engineering demonstration. It complements the [Gunnerus visualization](/projects/gunnerus-visualization/).
