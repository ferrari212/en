Published research

Connecting ship data across the lifecycle

Organize ship, sensor, and operational-context data across design and operation.

  • Data models
  • Digital twins
  • Web architectures

Engineering purpose

Ship information changes across design and operation. Different taxonomies and data sources can make it difficult to connect a digital model with sensor and operational context.

My contribution

I co-authored Open Framework for Digital Twin Ship Data: Case Studies on Handling of Multiple Taxonomies and Navigation Simulation with I. A. Fonseca and H. M. Gaspar. At NTNU, I contributed to the open framework for organizing vessel data across lifecycle stages.

Methods and implementation

The research considers lifecycle-oriented taxonomies, ship and sensor data, operational context, and navigation simulation. Related collaborative work examines open web architectures for maritime design and simulation.

Published result

The 2023 paper appeared in the International Journal of Maritime Engineering, 165(1), A23–A42. It received the RINA Wakeham Prize, described in my CV as the prize for the best general paper in the journal by an author aged 35 or under.
