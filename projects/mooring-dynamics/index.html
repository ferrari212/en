Mooring & floating-body dynamics

Investigate the response of floating systems and their mooring lines under environmental loading.

  • JavaScript
  • 6-DOF dynamics
  • Hydrodynamics

Engineering purpose

Floating structures respond to waves and constraints from their mooring systems. Representing those interactions helps explain coupled motion and line behaviour.

My contribution

During my part-time R&D work at NTNU, I developed six-degree-of-freedom dynamic simulation models for floating systems under environmental loading. I also contributed hydrodynamic and dynamic-response modelling within the wider ShipLab collaboration.

Methods and implementation

The work combines JavaScript, numerical dynamics, hydrodynamic models, and browser-based visualization. The linked Vessel.js example exposes parameters for exploring the response.

Demonstrated capability

The demonstration connects a floating body’s motion with an animated mooring system. It is an engineering visualization and experimentation tool; a project-specific verification process would be needed before using any model for design decisions.
