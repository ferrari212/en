ABOUT ME

Engineering is technical.
It’s also human.

I’m Felipe Ferrari de Oliveira, a naval and structural engineer working at the intersection of physical systems, scientific computing, and software.

My experience spans vessel design and construction, numerical simulation, operational data analysis, and engineering applications. I’m now developing my specialization in AI for maritime and industrial applications and preparing to begin a PhD at NTNU.
